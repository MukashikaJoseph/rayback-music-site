THE PROJECT

A more connected way to enjoy music.

RAYBACK Music 2.0 is an independent music application being developed to bring music discovery, playback, and personal organization into a cohesive experience. The project is being built and tested incrementally, with reliability and a thoughtful user experience at its core.
